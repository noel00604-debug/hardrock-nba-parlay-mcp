Rules change; the app's current rules prevail. Re-check before relying on any of this.

## 1. Void, pushed, or cancelled legs

House Rules Part A, Section 1.15: parlays with a cancelled or voided selection "will
stand as a parlay on the remaining selections, or as single in the case where one
selection in a two-selection parlay is cancelled." Part A, Section 3.2: potential
payouts "are reduced every time one of the selections is cancelled or pushed."

For a three-leg ticket: one void leg leaves a two-leg parlay; two void legs leave a
single. The rules do not state a different price for the surviving legs; the parlay
explainer (/sportsbook/parlay/) says odds are "recalculated as if the pushed leg was
not on the wager," and the push explainer (/sportsbook/push/) says "The odds get
recalculated based on your remaining selections."

## 2. Player props need the player dressed and on court

Part C, Section 3 (Basketball), Individual Player Props: "Players must be dressed and
see court-time for bets to have action. There is no time limit as to how long a player
must be on court. Push rules apply." A true DNP voids the leg, then rule 1 applies.
Quarter player props have action regardless of participation; they are out of scope on
day one (only full-game markets are supported).

## 3. Full-game markets include overtime

Part C, Section 3: "Markets include overtime unless otherwise stated. Quarters and 1st
Half markets do not include overtime unless otherwise stated." Spreads, totals,
moneylines, and full-game player props all include OT.

## 4. Postponement: 48-hour rule

Part C, Section 3: "If a game is interrupted or postponed and is not continued
within 48 hours of the initial start date, then betting will be cancelled." Totals
(Part B, Section 3): if a game is abandoned, bets are cancelled unless grading is
already determined.

## 5. Same-game legs must be a Same Game Parlay

Part A, Section 6 (Related Contingencies): outside the product features "purposed to
facilitate parlays within the same event, our system will block related contingency bets
when bet placement is attempted," and a bet accepted in error has its stake split
"across the related selections as singles." Part B, Section 16 defines the SGP as a
parlay with "multiple bet selections all occurring within the same event"; SGP Max lets
patrons parlay SGPs with other selections. Minimum two bets, maximum 20 in an SGP.

## 6. SGP pricing method is not published

No Hard Rock page states how an SGP is priced. The SGP page says only that more legs
produce longer odds. The app's combined quote is the only price; a product of leg prices
is not the SGP price. A pushed SGP leg is removed and the odds recalculated.

## 7. Cash out is at Hard Rock's discretion

Part A, Section 7.1 defines Cash Out as a settlement "prior to the conclusion of the bet
in exchange for a payout amount determined by Seminole Gaming based on current market
conditions"; 7.2 reserves the right to "amend, suspend or remove Cash Out availability
at any time". The SGP page: "early cashouts will not be available in every scenario".
Section 3.9: cash outs can be reversed when the bet would have been voided or adjusted.
A cashed-out ticket is recorded with the cash actually returned.

## 8. Results, grading delays, corrections, obvious errors

Part A, 3.10: grading may be delayed "until an official confirmation is published."
3.11: incorrect gradings may be corrected "anytime." 3.12: results come from the sport's
official governing body or Seminole Gaming providers; disciplinary results more than 24
hours after the event are not considered. 3.6 and 3.7: obvious errors can void a bet or
resettle a winner at the correct price. Independently sourced NBA results therefore
produce only a provisional outcome in this tool; the book's settlement is authoritative.

## 9. Bets cannot be changed after confirmation

Part A, 1.6: wagers "will not be able to be changed or voided by the patron upon
confirmation". Review the three legs, stake, and combined odds before confirming in the
app; this tool's rechecks list exists for that moment.

## 10. Florida specifics

- Operator: the Seminole Tribe of Florida d/b/a Seminole Gaming (Terms 1.2); regulated by
  the Seminole Tribal Gaming Commission under the 2021 Compact (Terms 1.3).
- Age and location: 21 or over and physically located in Florida while wagering (Terms
  3.1, 3.3.1, 10.8); geolocation by GeoComply (18.5).
- Responsible gaming: "In FL, call 1-833-PLAYWISE" (Florida landing page).
- Bonus Bets: stake excluded from winnings, expire seven days after award; promotions are
  cash bets only. The budget ledger tracks cash stake; bonus credits are recorded
  separately and never count as cash returned.
- Player Injury Insurance (NBA, 2026-10-01 to 2027-08-01 per the promo page): the
  parlay settles as a loser and a Bonus Bet refund up to the stake may follow when the
  only losing leg is the injured player's over; excludes unders, cash-outs, boosts.
  Record such a ticket as LOST with any bonus credited separately.

## 11. What is not in the rules

- No stat-correction cutoff time is named.
- No minimum game time for action beyond the 48-hour continuation rule.
- No maximum number of legs for a standard parlay is stated (the explainer mentions
  twelve or more).
