1. What this is. The NBA Three-Leg Parlay Analyst is a research and record-keeping tool
   for three-leg NBA parlays on Hard Rock Bet Florida. Its output is produced with AI
   assistance. It is not a prediction service, not financial advice, and not a
   guarantee of any outcome.

2. What it does not do. The software never places, cancels, or cashes out a bet, never
   deposits or withdraws money, never logs into a sportsbook, and never stores sportsbook
   credentials. Every wager is placed by you, in the Hard Rock Bet app, at your own
   decision and risk.

3. Your responsibilities. You are responsible for complying with the laws that apply to
   you, including Florida's requirement that sports bettors be 21 or older and
   physically in Florida, and with Hard Rock Bet's own terms and house rules. You are
   responsible for the accuracy of anything you type or confirm into the tool, including
   odds, stakes, and results. You agree not to share your account, not to probe other
   users' data, and not to use the server for anything other than its stated purpose.

4. Data and prices. Odds come from The Odds API and injury information from the official
   NBA injury report; both can be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. The software labels
   source timestamps and data modes, but you must verify the price and the selections in
   the Hard Rock app before confirming a wager. The combined price of a parlay is the one
   the app shows you, not a number computed by this tool.

5. Budget ledger. The daily cap is an application-level ledger that you control. It is
   not a sportsbook limit and does not stop you from betting more in the app. The tool
   records wagers you report, including ones above the cap, and flags them.

6. Accounts. You create an account on the server with an email and password. You can
   export or delete your data at any time from the account page. The operator may
   suspend or delete accounts that abuse the service or that stay unused for a long time,
   with notice where reasonably possible.

7. Availability and support. The service is provided as is, with no uptime commitment.

9. Responsible gambling. If gambling is a problem for you or someone you know, call
